# A11-K / Mind-Reply Brand System — 2026

## Brand hierarchy
**A11-K** is the public operating surface. **Mind-Reply** is the owner ecosystem. **Sofia Tech Ledger / Софийски Технологичен Регистър** is the regional intelligence product.

## Content promise
Make signals easier to understand, evidence easier to inspect, and next actions easier to execute.

## Voice
- Bilingual where useful for Bulgarian audiences.
- Precise, premium, human and evidence-led.
- Distinguish verified facts from emerging signals.
- Never claim a live connection, metric or deployment without runtime evidence.

## Visual language
- Deep near-black foundation with editorial whitespace.
- Soft blue for interaction and navigation.
- Green only for verified/live states.
- Large typography, thin borders, glass surfaces and restrained gradients.

## Motion language
- Ambient grid/glow movement: slow and subtle.
- Signal rings: continuous but low-frequency.
- Content: staged reveal and small hover lift.
- All motion must honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Core journey
**Signal → Context → Evidence → Decision → Execution → Verification.**
