02 / REVENUE CONTINUITY

Revenue survives the outage.

The commercial engine is deliberately model-optional. AI may accelerate discovery, qualification, content or support, but it cannot be a required dependency for a customer to buy, receive, renew or be recorded.

Hard boundaries
  • No model required for price calculation, order creation, entitlement, settlement, ledger posting, reconciliation or revenue reporting.
  • No autonomous price, contract, billing or money movement changes.
  • No fabricated customers, revenue, endorsements, scarcity or outcomes.
  • Every consequential external action remains explicitly owner-accountable.
