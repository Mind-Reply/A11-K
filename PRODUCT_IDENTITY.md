# A11-K — Flight Deck

A focused personal operating surface and private command layer for the Mind-Reply estate.

A11-K should feel like a calm instrument: it notices what matters, makes the next move clear, and never hides consequential actions behind automation.

## Who is this for?

The owner/operator who needs high-agency control, clear evidence, and deliberate dispatch across systems without noise.

## What human problem does it remove?

Scattered attention, opaque automation, and the inability to see and act on what actually matters in the moment.

## What is the smallest meaningful action?

Open the surface, see the current state, and take one clear, reversible next step.

## What happens when the system is wrong?

Consequential actions remain explicit. Automation does not hide decisions. Recovery paths and evidence are visible so the operator can intervene.
