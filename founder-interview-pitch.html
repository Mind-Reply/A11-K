A11-K · MEDIA / INTERVIEW

Founder interview pitch

For European technology, business, governance and future-of-work media.

Subject

“What if the next generation of AI systems were judged less by how impressive they sound—and more by how safely, honestly and usefully they operate?”

The story

Angel L. Krastev is building A11-K and MindReply around a simple operating principle: ambitious technology should increase human capability without increasing chaos. The public estate separates real systems from research fixtures and blocked claims, making evidence part of the product experience.

Interview angles
  • Why AI governance should be operational, not just paperwork.
  • How startups can publish ambitious work without inventing traction.
  • Security, reversibility and human oversight as competitive advantages.
  • What European companies can learn from evidence-first automation.
  • How AI/SI research can be presented responsibly before deployment.
Suggested questions
  1. What should companies prove before putting an AI system into production?
  2. Where does automation become dangerous without human ownership?
  3. Can governance improve speed rather than slow innovation?
  4. What would a genuinely balanced AI economy look like?
Editorial integrity

No invented interviews, customers, revenue, endorsements or official affiliations. A11-K can provide public evidence and demonstrations for verification.
