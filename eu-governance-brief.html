A11-K · EU FIRST · 23 AUGUST 2026

AI that increases capability without increasing chaos.

A11-K EU Governance Brief · v1.0 · discussion document, not an official EU position or legal advice.

Executive proposition

Europe can pursue ambitious AI adoption while making evidence, security, human oversight, reversibility and transparent accountability normal operating requirements.

Six operating principles
  1. Evidence before claims. Distinguish live, tested, simulated and blocked capabilities.
  2. Human control. Consequential decisions retain accountable human ownership and escalation.
  3. Secure by default. Minimum permissions, secret isolation, auditability and controlled external data flows.
  4. Reversible deployment. Prefer staged releases, checkpoints, rollback and test-mode commerce.
  5. Public legibility. Explain what a system does, what data it uses and what remains uncertain.
  6. Measure human outcomes. Evaluate safety, productivity, accessibility, resilience and distributional effects—not hype.
Policy conversation

A11-K invites European businesses, researchers, civil-society organisations, standards professionals and public institutions to challenge these principles, identify gaps and propose measurable improvements.
