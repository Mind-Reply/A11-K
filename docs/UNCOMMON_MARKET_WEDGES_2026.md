# Uncommon Market Wedges — 2026

Purpose: run small, evidence-led market experiments rather than generic country launches.

## EU
- Bulgaria: origin-market proof lab — recruit 3 reference customers, publish measurable before/after evidence, then export the case study.
- Germany: reliability wedge — lead with recovery, auditability and operational control; target engineering/ops leaders.
- France: design + sovereignty wedge — premium product experience with clear data/control story.
- Netherlands: interoperability wedge — partner with digital consultancies and infrastructure operators.
- Nordics: trust-by-design wedge — show failure handling and human override before feature breadth.
- Ireland: international SaaS wedge — use Ireland as an English-language EU enterprise gateway.
- Poland/Czechia/Romania: engineering-efficiency wedge — technical pilots with measurable deployment-time reduction.
- Italy/Spain/Portugal/Greece/Croatia: commerce/service wedge — customer communications and workflow automation first.
- Baltics: digital-native infrastructure wedge — small pilots, fast iteration, exportable references.
- Austria/Switzerland/Luxembourg/Belgium: premium-control wedge — regulated/enterprise buyers, evidence first.

## United States
- California: transparency/privacy-first proof; avoid broad claims.
- New York: enterprise governance + synthetic-content disclosure readiness.
- Texas: speed/operations wedge; quantify hours and failure reduction.
- Washington/Massachusetts: technical credibility; developer and research-led pilots.
- Virginia/Maryland: security, audit and controlled automation.
- Illinois/New Jersey/Florida: revenue operations and customer workflow pilots.

## Asia
- Japan: craftsmanship/reliability; invitation-only pilot.
- South Korea: speed + connected communications; mobile-first demonstration.
- Singapore: governance-as-product; enterprise proof pack.
- India: scale economics; benchmark cost per workflow/customer.
- UAE: premium command-center positioning; executive demo first.
- Saudi Arabia: transformation infrastructure; local partner route.
- Taiwan: deep-tech/industrial workflow wedge.
- Hong Kong: cross-border finance/commerce wedge.
- Indonesia/Philippines/Thailand/Vietnam/Malaysia: conversation + mobile commerce wedge.
- Israel: security/recovery engineering wedge.
- Turkey/Kazakhstan: Europe–Asia cross-border operations wedge.
- China: separate localization, legal and infrastructure strategy; no assumption of direct portability.

## Contact rule
Use direct named professional contacts only where the buyer, relevance and lawful outreach basis are verified. Prefer partner/referral/inbound routes where direct outreach is uncertain. Never purchase or scrape personal contact lists for bulk outreach.

## Experiment rule
Every country gets: one wedge, one buyer, one proof artifact, one 30-day test, one revenue hypothesis, one kill condition. Scale only after evidence.
