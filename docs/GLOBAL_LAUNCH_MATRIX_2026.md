# Global Launch Matrix 2026 — A.K.

Purpose: one global operating layer with local positioning, lawful contact rules, evidence gates, and measurable launch paths.

## Master rule

Do not publish or contact by geography merely because a market exists. Activate a country only when **product fit + lawful basis + proof + owner approval** are present.

Contact mode:
- **DIRECT_B2B** — named business/professional contact is genuinely useful; use public/professional contact routes, not scraped private data.
- **OPT_IN_ONLY** — require explicit marketing permission before promotional contact.
- **PARTNER_FIRST** — prefer local partner/channel/referral route.
- **PUBLIC_ONLY** — publish localized announcement and inbound CTA; no outbound list-building.
- **HOLD** — legal/rights/product localization evidence incomplete.

For any contact campaign: identity, lawful basis, source, consent/opt-out state, jurisdiction, purpose, suppression state, and timestamp must be recorded. Never buy or scrape personal-contact lists for promotional use.

## Contact activation ladder

1. **Inbound only** for uncertain jurisdictions or consumer campaigns.
2. **Partner-first** where local language, distribution, regulation or trust materially changes outcomes.
3. **Direct B2B** only for a narrow account list with a legitimate professional purpose and compliant outreach basis.
4. **Named decision-maker contact** only when the role is directly relevant; prefer public business channels or explicit opt-in.
5. **Stop/suppress** immediately on opt-out, unclear identity, legal restriction, or contradictory evidence.

## Measurement

Every activated country gets: impressions, qualified visits, CTA rate, booked conversations, qualified accounts, conversion, revenue, opt-out rate, complaint rate, and legal-review exceptions.

A country remains ACTIVE only if it produces either qualified demand or strategic proof. Otherwise it drops back to TEST or HOLD.

## Decision model

**ACTIVE:** strong fit + lawful route + proof.
**TEST:** plausible fit; limited budget; measure.
**PARTNER:** partner is the multiplier.
**HOLD:** unresolved law/rights/contact/localization issue.
**STOP:** adverse evidence or unacceptable risk.

## CEO gate

A.K. remains the release authority for consequential ownership, licensing, legal claims, certificates, policy commitments, high-impact contact campaigns, paid media commitments, billing, DNS and production changes.
