# Regional Announcement Intelligence

Purpose: run distinct, lawful launch and market-listening programs by country rather than one global message.

## Operating rule

Each market receives:
- local language and cultural framing;
- one primary commercial promise;
- one proof asset;
- two launch channels;
- one trusted-partner route;
- one organic “whisper” route based on genuine user advocacy, founder insight, expert commentary, community participation and earned media;
- one rumor-monitoring lane for detection and factual response;
- rights/privacy/platform review before monetization claims.

Do not fabricate rumors, testimonials, scarcity, endorsements, customers, press, market statistics or regulatory claims. Monitor chatter; respond with evidence.

## Announcement architecture

Every country gets five layers:

1. **Signal** — one sentence that creates curiosity without deception.
2. **Proof** — one working experience, benchmark, case, demo or visible artifact.
3. **Invitation** — one clear action with local pricing/currency and contact path.
4. **Whisper** — earned discussion from real users, experts, partners or founder commentary.
5. **Response** — monitored rumor/chatter log with evidence-backed corrections.

## Regional agent roles

Scout: local market signals, competitor moves, channel fit, language and cultural details.

Positioner: converts the product into the local promise without flattening the brand.

Announcer: prepares local launch assets, founder posts, partner copy and community announcements.

Whisperkeeper: tracks genuine word-of-mouth and sentiment; never fabricates social proof.

Compliance Keeper: checks current platform/consumer/privacy/advertising constraints and marks unknowns.

Revenue Keeper: watches conversion, CAC proxy, response rate, pipeline and revenue per market.

## Hourly monitoring

Every hour: deployment status, URL health, campaign delivery anomalies, major market signal changes, evidence freshness, legal/policy watchlist changes and severe rumor/incident signals.

Do not auto-publish claims, alter legal text, create synthetic testimonials, imitate local officials/media, or make consequential regulatory assertions without an authoritative source and CEO/authorized-human gate.
