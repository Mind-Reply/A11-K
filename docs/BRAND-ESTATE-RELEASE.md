## Release rule

Every public-facing surface must expose a stable brand identity, canonical role, release family, and evidence-backed source. A surface is not marked LIVE merely because source files exist: publication requires deployment evidence.

## Event/content rule

Events, campaigns, announcements and content bundles inherit the canonical brand of their owning product and retain a release/change identifier. External communications remain owner-approved actions.

## Domain rule

Domains and aliases are recorded as estate objects and reconciled against the actual Vercel/GitHub state. No domain is claimed as production without current routing/deployment evidence.
