05 / RECOVERY

Fail safely. Resume deliberately.

Recovery is part of autonomy: detect a broken path, preserve evidence, stop unsafe propagation, expose the smallest safe next action, and only resume after verification.

Detect

Build failures, broken links, missing assets, policy drift, dependency risk and deployment anomalies become evidence rather than hidden state.

Contain

Autonomous jobs fail closed. They do not mutate billing, credentials, DNS, production ownership or customer records to make a green dashboard.

Restore

Use the last verified artifact or rollback path. Re-run focused checks before declaring service recovered.

Resume

Resume only after the relevant gate is green and the evidence record identifies what changed, what was verified and what remains blocked.
