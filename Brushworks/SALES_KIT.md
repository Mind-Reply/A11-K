## Selling rules

* Lead with the customer outcome, not the internal architecture.
* Show the smallest useful proof before proposing a larger engagement.
* Never use fabricated revenue, client counts, testimonials, or deployment claims.
* Every CTA must have a concrete next action.
* Keep sensitive financial and customer data outside marketing exports.
