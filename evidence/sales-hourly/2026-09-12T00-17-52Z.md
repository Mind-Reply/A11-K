# A11 Global Revenue Pulse
Timestamp: 2026-09-12T00:17:52Z

## Revenue continuity
Revenue-critical paths MUST operate without generative AI, model APIs, inference credits, or assistant availability.
AI may accelerate research or drafting, but it is not a revenue dependency.

## Deterministic revenue loop
1. Discover — owned search, directories, partnerships, referrals and public distribution.
2. Qualify — explicit rules, forms and human review.
3. Offer — fixed packages, pricing and clear terms.
4. Checkout — hosted payment, invoice or approved bank settlement.
5. Deliver — documented fulfilment runbook and human fallback.
6. Renew — subscription/invoice calendar and renewal queue.
7. Refer — tracked referral mechanism with disclosed terms.
8. Measure — payment exports, ledger records and deterministic metrics.

## Hourly automated checks
- Verify public offer and checkout links are present.
- Check that each priority offer has a price, fulfilment owner and settlement route documented.
- Detect stale revenue opportunities and renewal dates when deterministic data is available.
- Record evidence without customer PII, payment data or secrets.
- Prepare internal CEO action queues only; do not contact prospects automatically.

## Commercial priority
Productised services; subscriptions; paid intelligence/data products; implementation and assurance; templates/operating systems; transparent partnerships/referrals; measured usage/transaction revenue.

## Guardrails
- No purchased lists, spam, deceptive identity, fake scarcity, fake endorsements or fabricated results.
- No secrets, payment data, customer PII or private ledger data.
- No automatic external messages from this workflow.
- No billing or price mutations.
- No unverified commercial claims.
- Every revenue-critical action must have a deterministic/manual fallback.

## AI independence gate
PASS means the offer can still be discovered, purchased/requested, fulfilled, recorded, supported and renewed when all AI providers are unavailable.

## Status
READY — automated preparation and verification only; human approval remains required for external outreach, contracts, payments and irreversible changes.
